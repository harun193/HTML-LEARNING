Flowers are one of the most beautiful gifts of nature. They make our surroundings colourful, attractive, and pleasant.
There are many kinds of flowers in the world, such as roses, lotuses, sunflowers, lilies, marigolds, and tulips.

They come in different shapes, sizes, and colours. Flowers have sweet fragrances that make us feel happy and refreshed. They are an important part of nature and help maintain the balance of the environment.

Bees, butterflies, and other insects collect nectar from flowers. Flowers are also used for decorating homes, gardens, weddings, and festivals. People give flowers to express love, friendship, respect, and happiness. Some flowers are used to make perfumes, medicines, and beauty products.

In Bangladesh, the rose, lotus, and water lily are very popular flowers.
Flowers teach us to spread beauty and happiness everywhere. 

  We should plant more flowering trees and protect them to keep our environment green, fresh, and beautiful.
