Prophet Muhammad (sul) was the last messenger of Allah and the founder of Islam. He was born in Makkah in 570 CE. He was known for his honesty, kindness, patience, humility, and generosity.

People called him Al-Amin, meaning "the trustworthy," because of his noble character. He taught people to worship one Allah, speak the truth, respect others, help the poor, and treat everyone with kindness and justice.
He showed great love and compassion for children, women, orphans, and needy people.
