Roses are one of the most beautiful and popular flowers in the world.
They are well known for their attractive colours, sweet fragrance, and elegant appearance.

Roses come in different colours, such as red, white, pink, yellow, orange, and purple.

Each colour has a special meaning. Red roses symbolize love and passion, white roses represent peace and purity, while yellow roses express friendship and happiness.

Roses are commonly used for decorating homes, gardens, weddings, and special occasions.
They are also given as gifts to express love, care, and appreciation.
